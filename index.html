Origami Tsuru!

A simple step by step to make your own origami crane (in spanish grulla)

Did you know?

Folding cranes is a gesture of sympathy, hope, or healing. Used at weddings for wishing longevity or to support those struggling with illness

Tsuru, (The Crane), has always been a strong symbol of success and good fortune in Japanese culture, and when folded into origami, it is believed that one’s “heart's desire” will come true.
